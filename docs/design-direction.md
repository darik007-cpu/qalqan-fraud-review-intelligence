# QALQAN — investigation and ledger workspace

QALQAN is a light, calm banking investigation workspace. Bottle green, soft white and restrained rounded forms accompany serif editorial headings and readable sans-serif evidence. Its composition, navigation, typography and working views are independent of STRATUM's dark engineering console.

## Composition

A compact Qalqan wordmark and monogram introduce the analyst workspace. Unnumbered tabs lead to Overview, Investigate, Transfer network, Policy, Journal, Data and Geography. The active tab is a filled green pill. The same visible tabs scroll horizontally on narrow screens; there is no hidden navigation drawer.

The overview pairs a considered statement with an actual snapshot ledger. Observed transfer value, record and account counts, review signals and the first three supplied records are computed from the active graph. Uploaded snapshots retain their provenance and do not receive an invented calendar date. The monogram seal is a visual identity element, not a certification, security rating or regulatory approval.

Below the opening ledger, a short vertical workflow sits beside a contained relationship preview. The Three.js graph supports the evidence workflow rather than forming a full-width cinematic hero. A separate local-context note links to existing national services and identifies the product idea as a hypothesis.

The Transfer network page makes the financial ledger primary. It opens with all supplied transfer records, an account filter and an independently scrollable table. Selecting an account in either the ledger or the adjacent 3D view filters the records; a record opens its investigation. The contained graph retains account selection, timeline, spatial separation, motion controls, view reset and export. At smaller widths the ledger and supporting graph stack.

Investigate preserves the searchable review queue and an evidence dossier. Policy comparisons expose workload and known-outcome limits. The journal records editable local case reasoning. The data workspace keeps validation and provenance visible. Geography is a separately owned city-reference view and does not place accounts or customers on a map.

## Foundation

- Canvas: `#f6f8f4`; white working surfaces.
- Primary ink: `#223c31`; bottle-green action: `#185d45`.
- Sage-tinted secondary areas and quiet financial rules.
- Relationship stage: dark bottle green `#0a2119`, coordinated with the actual Three.js scene.
- Headings: Georgia, with a standard serif fallback; no remote font request.
- Controls, evidence and numeric readouts: locally bundled Inter.
- Maximum content width: 1440 px with generous responsive gutters.
- Restrained 8–14 px corners; no glow or decorative financial performance claims.

Serif type gives the product an editorial character; tabular sans-serif numerals retain clarity in amounts and scores. Review states use text labels together with amber, rose and green. Scores remain review priorities, never probabilities of fraud.

## Interaction and acceptance

All navigation and controls preserve visible keyboard focus. The graph's focus ring is inset against the green stage. Investigation details are sticky only on large screens; tables scroll locally and details stack on phones. Reduced-motion styles preserve the functional interface.

Domain logic, imports, case persistence and the SSR review harness remain intact. Final regression tests and the production build accompany this redesign; browser composition is checked separately by the integrating agent. QALQAN is a standalone local demonstration with no live bank connection, payment control or certified security claim. No Figma tooling is required.
